أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ
Transliteration: Asbahna wa asbahal-mulku lillah.
Meaning: We have reached the morning and at this very time all sovereignty belongs to Allah.
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا
Transliteration: Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna.
Meaning: O Allah, by Your leave we have reached the morning and by Your leave we have reached the evening.
